## Featured project: Estiqra

**Real estate intelligence, built from evidence.**

Estiqra brings property evidence, pricing, feasibility, and investment reporting into a connected workspace for development, investment, and advisory teams. AI helps organize and analyze the evidence; people review the assumptions and the final output.

**Current public stage:** early access, with a Dubai pilot product preview.

## Estiqra Operate

**One suite for the operational side of property.**

Estiqra Operate brings Leasing, Hospitality, Facilities Management, and developer-sales CRM into four focused workspaces. It follows the day-to-day work of property teams: agreements, rent and collections, hotel stays, maintenance, sales inventory, and buyer balances.

**Current public stage:** a working local prototype, demonstrated with fictional data.

[Explore the Operate showcase and screenshots](https://github.com/mosaradwan-star/estiqra-operate-showcase)

## Aqar · AqarFlow

**From property discovery to brokerage follow-through.**

Aqar connects Arabic and English property search with owner submissions, brokerage CRM, offers, and planning and investment analysis. Its initial market context is Jenin and the West Bank.

**Current public stage:** a local product build, demonstrated with fictional or blank records.

[Explore the Aqar showcase and screenshots](https://github.com/mosaradwan-star/aqar-showcase)
