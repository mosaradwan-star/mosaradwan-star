## Featured project: Estiqra

**Real estate intelligence, built from evidence.**

Estiqra brings property evidence, pricing, feasibility, and investment reporting into a connected workspace for development, investment, and advisory teams. AI helps organize and analyze the evidence; people review the assumptions and the final output.

**Current public stage:** early access, with a Dubai pilot product preview.
